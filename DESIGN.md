# MOSSBOUND · 숲의 수호자

An immersive game lobby, not a marketing page. The actual procedural 3D forest is the central visual and becomes the playable world. Original rounded low-poly woodland spirits, miniature moss islands, sunlit foliage, paper-like controls. No Pokémon assets, names, sprites or source code are reused.

Palette: forest #244b39, moss #72965a, mist #edf2e8, paper #fffdf5, ember #e88b4c, muted #7d897b. Typography: rounded Korean system sans and heavy compact Latin wordmark. A restrained navigation rail, wide scene, and one expedition panel establish the main hierarchy. Friendly green rings indicate selected companions. Responsive mobile layout preserves the world and start action; reduce decorative movement for reduced-motion users.

Original companions: 모루 (moss-covered wooden guardian), 루미 (floating lantern spirit with petal wings), 도토 (acorn-bodied mushroom wanderer). Each has its own silhouette, attack, stats, and ability.

The moonlit region uses blue ambient light (#53697f sky) and light captions; the day region retains the original moss palette. Foreground trees fade during combat so incoming enemies remain visible. Growth at levels 4 and 7 enlarges companions and adds luminous rings. The original thorn-crowned boss, 가시왕 그루몽, has a muted plum health bar that appears only during its fight. Keep battle feedback legible and reserve effects for attacks, growth, and player actions.
